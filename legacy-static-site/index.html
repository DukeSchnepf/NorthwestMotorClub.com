About Us

Where The PNW Car Scene Comes Together

Northwest Motor Club is more than just a car club — we're a community. Since 2018, we've been bringing together car enthusiasts from across the Seattle metropolitan area for meets, cruises, and good times.

From JDM legends to Euro muscle, stance builds to track monsters — if you're passionate about cars, you belong here. We host regular meets across the Eastside, from Issaquah to Woodinville, Redmond to Edmonds.
